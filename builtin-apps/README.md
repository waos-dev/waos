# Built-in apps

WaOS's own apps, written as programs over `wasdk` as any program is, and kept here so they grow with the project and show what it lacks: each is an app a person uses, where `examples/` holds programs that each show one capability.

- **Notes** (`notes`): a library of notes the app keeps in its own `/data`, and text files from anywhere edited in place beside them, in WinUI's Fluent look, a sidebar of them beside the note, or a pane over it at a phone's width, searched, deleted into Recently Deleted, and imported and exported as text files.
- **Video Player** (`video-player`): a library of the videos in the folders its user adds, with thumbnails, lengths and how far each was watched, by folder, Continue Watching and search; plays one, or a file or link, with the player's controls, subtitles and audio tracks, in Fluent's look.
- **Terminal** (`terminal`): the user's login shell on a machine they picked for it, in a pseudo-terminal of the machines interface, a window to a session; its screen kept by `vt100` and drawn as a grid of cells in the platform's monospace font, with scrollback, selection, copy and paste, the mouse for programs that ask, and the system's light or dark look.
- **Model Viewer** (`model-viewer`): a glTF viewer written without Waft, the scene drawn with wgpu and the panel with egui, fed from a view's events by glue of its own; opens a `.glb` or a self-contained `.gltf`, lights it by glTF's metal and roughness model, orbits, pans and zooms, plays its animations and skins, and shows it shaded, as a wireframe or by its normals; it opens with a sample made in code.
- **Camera** (`camera`, the package `camera-app`): the camera's picture live in the window, drawn through one of six looks by a shader of the app's own on its own WebGPU device; photos the system takes, developed in the look and kept in the app's `/data`, viewed large, exported through the save dialog and deleted; it says why there is no picture while WaOS asks for the camera, when it is refused and when there is none, in Fluent's dark look.
- **Reader** (`reader`): a feed reader: RSS and Atom feeds followed by address, fetched over `wasdk::http` at launch, every half hour and on demand; their items listed newest first with pictures the system's codecs decode, read in the app as headings, paragraphs, lists, code and pictures, their links opened in the system's browser; read marks kept in `/data`, and a notification of new items where the user turned it on, in Fluent's look.
- **Synth** (`synth`): an instrument played at once: a keyboard for the mouse, several fingers and the computer's keys, six presets made with `fundsp`, five sliders, Sustain and a scope of what plays, in Fluent's look; its sound is made in the program and written a short lead ahead of the platform's audio graph through a stream input. Run it `--release`.
- **Documents** (`typst`, the package `documents`): a live document editor built on Typst: its markup written on one side, coloured as source, and its typeset pages on the other, set by Typst's own compiler inside the program, on a worker so the window stays live, as it is typed and drawn at the screen's density; problems underlined and listed with their line; `.typ` files opened and saved and a PDF exported through the system's dialogs; one side at a time at a phone's width, in Fluent's look. Run it `--release`.
- **Inspector** (`inspector`): what the programs on this device do with what they were allowed, as the inspector role lets it see: the programs, those that used something guarded lately first, and for the one chosen a report of its last seven days, then live panels of its network, files, access, output and what it uses now, in Fluent's look at a desktop's width and Cupertino's at a phone's. Its icon is WaOS's default program icon until one is drawn.

Each runs with `cargo waos run -p <name>` against a WaOS built from this workspace, and is run by the engine's tests (`crates/waos/tests/`).

Each app's `icon.png` is rendered from the `icon.svg` beside it, which is the source to edit; the eight are drawn as one family, in one light and finish, for other apps' icons to sit beside. On macOS, `qlmanage -t -s 1024 -o <folder> icon.svg` renders it as `<folder>/icon.svg.png`, which is then saved without its alpha channel, as an RGB PNG of 1024 pixels on a side (with Pillow: `Image.open(path).convert("RGB").save("icon.png")`). Model Viewer's `icon.svg` is written by its `icon.py` (`python3 icon.py icon.svg`), which computes the sphere's facets and their shading.
