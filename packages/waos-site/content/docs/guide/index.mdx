title: Building the app
description: What the platform gives an app, one page each, with wasdk and with Waft.
---
Each page says what the platform gives, then how a program uses it with wasdk, then with Waft, then where it is missing, and links its interface in the [reference](/docs/reference). With Waft, the platform's own objects come from `app.platform()`, and the types they take, such as `OpenOptions` and `Notification`, from `waft::ui`.
