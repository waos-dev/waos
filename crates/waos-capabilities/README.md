# waos-capabilities

The capabilities a provider outside the engine serves (machines, sound devices, the apps an inspector sees; fonts through valo's font manager), as traits it implements, and what its user lets each app use; and the WIT's value types as plain Rust (`wit`), generated by `cargo xtask sync-wit`.

- **Takes**: serde, for what is kept and sent between processes; futures; valo's font manager and web-audio-api, re-exported. Nothing of the engine's, wasmtime's or wasm's.
- **Provides**: the capability traits, `InspectedApps` among them, `ProgramEnvironment` and `ProgramGrants`; `wit`, the records, variants, enums and flags of `wasdk:platform`, `wasdk:media` and `wasdk:inspect` at the engine's paths for them, with `Clone`, `Debug`, `PartialEq` and serde's derives, which the engine converts to its own with `From`.
- **Used by**: the engine, and the providers an embedding app gives it.
